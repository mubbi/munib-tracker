### 1. Verdict

The redesign is a real improvement and the highest-value problems from the audit are fixed in code: the core loop (tracker order, one-tap mark, status sheet that stays open, home hero mark), Qaza friction, token-level contrast holes, the web focus ring and focus loss, toasts, destructive confirms, the vault recovery sheet, the watch and Wear OS accidental-mark problems. Of the 49 original High findings I count **42 Closed, 7 Partly, 0 Open**; 4 of the 42 are closed in code only and have never run on a device.

It is **not finished and should not be signed off as complete**. The tracker itself says so in the rows, but not in the gates: Waves 5 and 6 are marked "Passed" while 14 Phase-5 tasks are "In progress", Phases 6–9 are almost entirely "Pending", and the lane-sweep matrix is 240/240 "Pending". The gates measure green tests, not design completion. The sweeps that were skipped are exactly where the remaining defects sit: unnamed dialogs, Arabic language tagging, raw text inputs, leftover spinners, and status colour reused as text on the home schedule. The pass also introduced at least two visible regressions (mid-word label breaks on the Zikr hub; "0 hadith" on offline collections).
