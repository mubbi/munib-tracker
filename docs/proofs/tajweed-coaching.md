# Proof log: Tajweed error coaching (NF-3.15, D3 / D20 / D24 / D25)

- **Feature:** a coaching plan built from the person's own Qur'an practice. It lists the tajweed sounds that the ayahs they're working on **contain** (never "you made a mistake"), with example words from their own recitation, the rule explanation, a Quran Lab sound drill for the three validated heads (qalqalah, ghunna, gemination) and listen-and-repeat cards for everything else. Progress is "times practised" and "next practice" only: no score, percentage or match rate is ever shown.
- **Code:** `apps/app/src/lib/tajweed-coaching/*` (rule → category map, markup → study-word mapping, plan builder, spacing / sanitize / merge, refresh, actions, evidence), `apps/app/src/stores/tajweed-coaching-store.ts` (blob `tajweed_coaching`), `apps/app/src/components/tajweed-coaching/*`, the "Coaching" tab on `/learn-quran/tajweed`, the "Practise these sounds" card on `/quran/practice/summary`, the practice route `/learn-quran/tajweed/coach/[item]`, and an optional line in the revision reminder. Rule explanations: `packages/shared/src/content/tajweed-coaching-guide.ts`.
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-24.
- **Scripture in the UI:** none typed. Example words are Quran.com study words resolved **by reference** (`surah:ayah:wordIndex` → `api/quran-words.ts`, cache-first) with the reciter's word audio. The guide copy names letters with single Arabic letters only; a unit test (`tajweed-coaching-guide.test.ts`) fails if any run of two or more Arabic letters (a typed word) appears in it.

## 2. Rule explanations (`tajweed-coaching-guide.ts`)

Classical works cited by title (the explanations are the standard teaching of these works for the riwayah of Hafs from 'Asim by way of al-Shatibiyyah; the review checked the rule content against the named chapters, **edition and page numbers were not recorded** because no single printed edition was consulted page by page — an open item for a human pass):

- **al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah** (Ibn al-Jazari, d. 833 AH) — sections on the letters' qualities (sifat), qalqalah, ghunnah on a doubled noon / meem, and starting on hamzat al-wasl.
- **Tuhfat al-Atfal** (Sulayman al-Jamzuri) — chapters on noon sakinah and tanween, meem sakinah, the doubled noon and meem, the lam of al-, and madd.
- **al-Nashr fi al-Qira'at al-'Ashr** (Ibn al-Jazari) — idgham, madd and the readers' differences.
- **al-Tamhid fi 'Ilm al-Tajwid** (Ibn al-Jazari) and **al-Ri'ayah** (Makki ibn Abi Talib, d. 437 AH) — the doubled (mushaddad) letter.
- **Hirz al-Amani (al-Shatibiyyah)** (al-Shatibi, d. 590 AH) — chapter on madd and qasr (lengths per reader).
- **Hidayat al-Qari ila Tajwid Kalam al-Bari** (al-Marsafi, d. 1409 AH) — a modern summary used for the Hafs lengths and counts.
