# Proof log: Wasiyyah (Islamic will) builder (NF-3.9)

- **Feature:** a guided, versioned, printable Islamic will: testator + shahadah, funeral wishes, debts and obligations (imported from NF-3.8), trusts, bequests ≤ ⅓, guardians and executor, distribution by the law of inheritance, two witnesses.
- **Code:** `apps/app/src/lib/wasiyyah/*` (`wasiyyah.ts` rules, `review.ts`, `document.ts`, `wasiyyah-store.ts`), `apps/app/src/lib/printable/*`, `apps/app/src/components/wasiyyah/*`, routes `apps/app/src/app/wasiyyah/*`. Madhhab rows in `packages/shared/src/fiqh/differences.ts` (`wasiyyah.debtsToAllah`, `wasiyyah.noHeirs`). Copy: `apps/app/scripts/i18n/wasiyyah-i18n.json`.
- **Storage:** vault document `wasiyyah` only (D2).
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23. Refs checked as in `docs/proofs/debts.md` §0 (bundled Qur'an corpus; `fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1`, Muslim paired by `arabicnumber`).

## 2. Section 1 — declaration of faith (rendered from the dataset)

- Text: `SALAH_GUIDE_PHRASES` entry `wudu-dua` (`packages/shared/src/content/salah-guide-phrases.ts`): Arabic, transliteration, translation, reference "Sahih Muslim 234".
- Verified: Muslim 234 (Abd al-Baqi) = corpus `muslim:554` (`arabicnumber` 234.02): "…I testify that there is no god but Allah, the One, there is no associate with Him and I testify that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger." (`muslim:553` = 234.01, same hadith without the wording.) Corpus `muslim:234` is unrelated.
- The dataset's English translation is printed only in English or side-by-side output; other locales print Arabic + transliteration (no hand-translated shahadah in 23 catalogs).

## 4. Funeral wishes (section 2)

Neutral wording only: ghusl, kafan and Janazah Salah per the Sunnah; burial in a Muslim cemetery; "keep the funeral simple, without practices not taught in the Sunnah" — no list of named innovations (scholars differ on several). Linked to the existing Janazah guide (`/janazah`, its own sources).

## 5. Disclaimer (legal review item, plan §4.3)

"Not legally binding in many jurisdictions — have it executed and registered according to your local law. This tool records your wishes; for rulings on your situation, consult a qualified scholar." Shown on the overview, the review step and printed on every document.

## 6. Reminders and privacy

- "Review your wasiyyah" every N months (3/6/12/24, default 12, stored in the vault) and when open "I owe" balances move by more than 20 % since the last review; overdue nudges repeat weekly, never daily. Generic text, §11 tier 4, `notificationPrefs.wasiyyahReview`, paused during excused periods.
- Export (D30): native renders a PDF with `expo-print` (`printToFileAsync`) into the cache, shares it, and deletes it when the share sheet closes; "Preview / print" opens the system print dialog (`printAsync`, which also offers Save as PDF) without writing a file. A binary built before `expo-print` was added falls back to sharing a temporary HTML file (also deleted afterwards). Web prints from an in-memory iframe. Hidden on TV.
