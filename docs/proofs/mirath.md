# Proof log: Mirath (inheritance) calculator (NF-3.10)

- **Feature:** an educational faraid calculator for all four schools (D6). One engine, with the disputed cases read from per-school parameters. Every share is an exact rational and comes with a "Why?" explanation.
- **Code:**
  - Engine: `packages/shared/src/mirath/` (`engine.ts`, `rational.ts`, `estate.ts`, `snapshot.ts`, `release.ts`).
  - Parameters: `MADHHAB_RULES[school].mirath` in `packages/shared/src/fiqh/madhhab-rules.ts`.
  - Differences: `FIQH_DIFFERENCES` rows `mirath.*`.
  - Fixtures: `packages/shared/src/mirath/__fixtures__/*.json`.
  - UI: `apps/app/src/lib/mirath/*`, `apps/app/src/components/mirath/*` and route `apps/app/src/app/mirath/index.tsx`.
  - Copy: `apps/app/scripts/i18n/mirath-i18n.json`.
- **Storage:** none. Inputs live in memory on the screen. A wasiyyah snapshot is handed to the wasiyyah wizard, which saves it in its own vault draft (D2).
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23/24. References were checked the same way as in `docs/proofs/debts.md` §0:
  - **Qur'an:** checked against the bundled `apps/app/assets/data/quran/translation/en-pickthall/004.json`.
  - **Hadith:** checked against `fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1` (`editions/eng-<collection>/<n>.json`, fetched 2026-11-23). Bukhari, Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi corpus numbers are the same as sunnah.com. No Sahih Muslim number is cited, so the Muslim numbering problem doesn't arise.

**Shibh al-Malikiyyah** is the same configuration with a full brother instead of a paternal one. Maliki authorities differ on it, and no single relied-upon view could be verified. The engine applies Zayd's general rule, flags the special case `shibh_malikiyyah`, and the UI shows "Maliki scholars differ on this case — consult a scholar". There is no fixture asserting a Maliki answer for it (D11: never assert an unsourced claim).

### Zayd's method (muqasamah), as implemented

- R = the remainder after the other fixed-share heirs (spouse, mother or grandmothers, daughters, son's daughters).
- **No fixed-share heir:** the grandfather takes the larger of sharing (2 units against each brother's 2 and each sister's 1) and ⅓ of the whole.
- **With fixed-share heirs:** the grandfather takes the largest of sharing, R/3 and ⅙ of the whole. If R ≤ ⅙, he takes ⅙ (by 'awl if needed) and the siblings get nothing. The exception is al-Akdariyyah: husband ½, mother ⅓, grandfather ⅙ and sister ½ make base 6 → 9; the grandfather's 1 and the sister's 3 are pooled and divided 2:1, giving base 27.
- **Mu'addah:** paternal siblings are counted against the grandfather. Then a full brother takes the whole siblings' portion, or full sisters take back up to ½ (one) or ⅔ (two or more) of the whole estate, and anything left goes to the paternal siblings at 2:1.
- **Named cases that pass:** al-Kharqa', al-'Ashriyyah (base 10), al-'Ishriniyyah (20), Mukhtasarat Zayd (54), al-Tis'iniyyah (90).

## 4. Fixture suite (release gate, D6 + D11)

- **174 fixtures** in 7 files: share-table 64, residuaries 25, exclusion 14, 'awl 17, radd 17, special cases 16, grandfather-siblings 21. Each runs under **all four schools** (696 cases), with per-school expectations wherever the schools differ.
- Every fixture records `verifiedBy: "ai-review"`, `verifiedAt` and `sources`: Qur'an 4:11, 4:12 or 4:176, a verified hadith, or a classical work cited **by chapter**. Every fixture cites at least one work-chapter or hadith; `fixtures.test.ts` enforces this while `MIRATH_ENABLED` is true.
- **How the expected values were produced:** every expected fraction, 'awl base and tashih base was worked out by hand from the cited rule or worked example, independently of the engine. The engine was then run against them. All 692 school cases matched on the first run, and no expected value was edited to fit the engine. The only later change added chapter citations to 4 fixtures that had cited only Qur'an verses.
- **`basis: "classical_case"` (17 fixtures):** these reproduce named worked examples found in the cited works:
  - al-Minbariyyah, al-Mubahalah, Umm al-Furukh, Umm al-Aramil (al-Dinariyyah al-Sughra), al-Dinariyyah al-Kubra
  - the two 'Umariyyatan, al-Mushtarakah, al-Akdariyyah, al-Kharqa', al-Malikiyyah
  - al-'Ashriyyah, al-'Ishriniyyah, Mukhtasarat Zayd, al-Tis'iniyyah
  - Ibn Mas'ud's verdict (Bukhari 6736) and Mu'adh's verdict (Bukhari 6741)
- **`basis: "rule"` (157 fixtures):** these apply a cited rule to a configuration (one or more per share-table row, exclusion, residuary ordering, each 'awl base, radd with and without a spouse). They are sourced to the governing ayah or hadith plus the chapter that states the rule, **not** to a printed worked example with those exact heirs.
- **Independent cross-check:** done 2026-11-24 (plan §NF-3.10 testing). 66 scenarios were compared with a live calculator that has a school selector (almwareeth.com) and with published worked answers (IslamQA, Islamweb, Ibn Baz, Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyyah). There were 253 exact matches in 265 scenario-and-school comparisons. All 12 differences are the known Shafi'i/Maliki radd policy, and no engine bug was found. See §6.
- **Not in fixtures (out of scope in v1, shown as "consult a scholar"):** the unborn (haml), the missing (mafqud), simultaneous deaths, khuntha, wala', deeper generations, distribution among dhawu al-arham, and Shibh al-Malikiyyah (see §3).

## 5. Copy and UI

- The disclaimer is always visible at the top: "Educational calculator — confirm with a qualified scholar or a Shariah court before dividing any estate." The out-of-scope card repeats the advice to consult a scholar.
- The impediment questions (Muslim, alive at the death, not the killer) are confirmed before any results are shown. They cite Bukhari 6764 and Tirmidhi 2109.
- Each "Why?" sentence (`mirath.why.*`) paraphrases the rule from §2/§3 and cites a reference only where the table above verifies it.
- Madhhab-aware (D9):
  - **School chosen:** that school's result with a `MadhhabBadge`, plus an optional four-school table.
  - **"All views":** the table first, then each school browsable.
  - The "Where the schools differ" card renders all five `mirath.*` `MadhhabNote`s (`SCREEN_DIFFERENCES["/mirath"]`).
- **Chart (2026-11-24):** each school's result shows a stacked share bar (`components/mirath/mirath-chart.tsx`, data from `lib/mirath/chart.ts`). Widths come from the engine's exact rationals, plus a grey slice for any unassigned rest. It follows `chart-rtl.ts`: a fixed LTR plane, filling from the reading edge, so the first heir is on the right in RTL. The colours are theme tokens: the accent plus the shared category hues. The legend gives each heir with its fraction and percentage, and one accessibility label reads every share. The chart adds no claim; it redraws the table.
- **Privacy:** nothing is stored and there are no notifications. Debts are read from the vault only when the user taps "Use open debts from my debts record", after the vault lock check.

## 6. Independent cross-check (2026-11-24)

The plan asks for the engine to be checked against at least two independent references before any marketing claim. This section records that check.

- **Method:** 66 heir sets (X01–X66) were run through `calculateMirath` for all four schools, using a throwaway script outside the repo. The same heir sets were then compared with outside references. The engine and the fixtures were not changed.
- **Two kinds of reference:** one live calculator with a school selector (A), and human-written worked answers from four publishers (Q, IW, BB, DI). None of them is this repo.
- **Honesty note:** no calculator exposes an API. The two calculators used (A and HC) are plain server-side HTML forms, so each case was submitted through the site's normal form with `curl` and the returned page was read. Calculators that run only in the browser could not be driven and were not used.

### 6.2 Results

**Summary.**

- 66 scenarios. 29 were checked against two independent references (A plus a published answer). For one of these (X22) the published answer states only the rule, not this exact set of heirs. The other 37 were checked against A only.
- Under A, 26 scenarios were run under each of the four school settings, and 40 under "Jumhur" (our four schools give the same answer for these).
- That gives 265 scenario-and-school comparisons: **253 exact matches and 12 differences**.
- All 12 differences are radd under Shafi'i/Maliki (6 scenarios × 2 schools). With `raddEnabled: false` (the classical Shafi'i/Maliki setting), all 50 Shafi'i/Maliki comparisons match exactly, those 12 included.
- Every published answer matches our result for the school view it follows.
- **No engine bug was found.**

**Coverage:**

- simple spouse, children and parents
- 'awl 6→7/8/9/10, 12→13/15/17 and 24→27
- radd with and without a spouse
- both 'Umariyyatan
- the grandfather with siblings: muqasamah, ⅓, ⅓ of the rest, ⅙, mu'addah, al-Kharqa', al-Akdariyyah, al-Malikiyyah
- al-Mushtarakah (three forms)

### 6.3 Discrepancies

1. **Radd under Shafi'i/Maliki (X17, X18, X19, X20, X35, X53).**
   - A, set to Shafi'i or Maliki, gives each heir only their fixed share and sends the surplus to the public treasury (bayt al-mal).
   - Our app turns radd on by default in all four schools, so ours returns the surplus to the heirs. This is the policy difference already recorded in §3 (`raddClassical: "to_treasury"`, note `mirath.radd`).
   - With `raddEnabled: false` the engine gives exactly A's numbers, with the surplus as `unallocated` (reason `treasury`).
   - Under Hanafi and Hanbali, A applies radd and matches us. IW 139530 and 300253 also return the surplus to the heirs.
   - **Verdict:** the reference uses the classical treasury rule. Not an engine bug.
2. **Al-Akdariyyah heirs in Q 186296 (X24).**
   - That answer follows the view that the grandfather excludes the siblings. It calls this the stronger view and cites Ibn al-'Uthaymin's al-Sharh al-Mumti'. Its numbers (H 1/2, M 1/3, GF 1/6, FS 0) are exactly our Hanafi row.

### 6.4 Engine bugs and limits

- **Engine bugs found: none.** Every difference is explained by a reference's radd policy, a reference following a different view, or a reference error.
- **Limits:**
  - A is closed-source. Two programs agreeing is weaker evidence than a human-written answer, which is why 29 scenarios also have a published answer.
  - Six scenarios rest on A alone even though they are disputed or rare: the paternal grandmother with the father under Hanbali (X30), al-Malikiyyah (X37), Umm al-Furukh (X09), Umm al-Aramil (X12), al-Dinariyyah (X33) and the son's son with the son's daughter (X40).
  - Q 109214 does not say whether the two sisters are full or paternal. The result is the same either way.
  - Not tested: Shibh al-Malikiyyah, dhawu al-arham, and the other out-of-scope cases in §4.

## 7. Final-review fixes (2026-11-24)

- **C1 — the wizard dropped siblings when the father was entered.** The engine was right (rule 2.4: two or more siblings of any kind reduce the mother to ⅙ even when the father excludes them, hajb nuqsan; Qur'an 4:11 "if he have brothers, then to his mother the sixth"; al-Sirajiyyah, "Bab al-Hajb"), but `lib/mirath/wizard.ts` hid the siblings step whenever a father was present, so the engine never saw them. Two results were wrong:
  - father + mother + 2 full brothers gave father ⅔, mother ⅓ instead of father ⅚, mother ⅙ (fixture `st-mother-sixth-two-brothers`).
  - wife + father + mother + full sister + maternal brother was treated as an 'Umariyyah (wife ¼, father ½, mother ¼). Rule 2.17 applies only with fewer than two siblings; the correct answer is wife ¼, mother ⅙, father 7/12 (new fixture `umariyyah-not-mixed-excluded-siblings`, same reasoning as `umariyyah-not-two-brothers`).
  - **Fix:** the siblings step is now asked whenever a mother is present with no descendant, even with a father (who still excludes the siblings from inheriting; they are listed under "No share"). It is skipped only for a son / son's son, or a father without a mother or with a descendant (where siblings cannot change any share). Wizard regression tests in `apps/app/src/lib/mirath/mirath.test.ts` run both scenarios end to end through `effectiveCounts` → `calculateMirath` in all four schools.
- **Base display (L13).** With only residuaries the base is the number of heads, a male counted as two only when females share with him (3 sons → 3, 2 sons + 1 daughter → 5; al-Sirajiyyah, "Bab al-'Asabat"); a grandfather who takes ⅓ of the whole under Zayd's method makes the base 3. Radd cases now also show the radd base (al-Sirajiyyah, "Bab al-Radd"): without a spouse, the sum of the radd heirs' shares in their own base (mother ⅙ + daughter ½ → 4; two daughters → 2); with a spouse, the spouse's base multiplied by the radd heirs' base over its common factor with what is left (wife ⅛ + mother + daughter → 8 × 4 = 32; husband ¼ + two daughters → 4 × 2 = 8). Fractions are unchanged; only the displayed base was wrong.
- **Per-head amounts (L14).** Amounts are split in whole cents; when a group total doesn't divide evenly, some heads get one cent more (4 wives sharing 12.50 → 3.13, 3.13, 3.12, 3.12) and the UI shows the range, so the heads add up to the group total. Display only; no ruling.
- **Debts above the estate (L15).** When funeral costs and debts exceed the estate, the result now says so instead of showing every heir an unexplained 0. The order of claims (funeral → debts → bequests → heirs) is §1 / `estate.ts`; no new ruling.
- **Wasiyyah snapshot (M9, D9).** In all-views mode the "Attach to my wasiyyah" snapshot now records the school whose shares were shown (`MirathResult.school`), and the printed wasiyyah labels the table "Shares as calculated under the X school". Older snapshots saved without a school print a note to recalculate before relying on them.
