# Proof log: Accountability partner (NF-3.17)

- **Feature:** a `partner` circle (2–5 members, joined by invite). Each member keeps a private daily goal on the device (all 5 fard / my wird / Qur'an X pages / custom) that is never sent anywhere. The only shared signal is `goal_done: { done: true }` for a local date, shown as dots: "done" or "no signal". Members can make an anonymous dua nudge ("Someone made dua for you") and send one fixed gentle reminder, which the receiver sees only after their own local Isha. Members can pause sharing or leave silently. There are no percentages, totals, streaks or leaderboards.
- **Code:** `apps/app/src/lib/circles/partner.ts`, `partner-goal-store.ts`, `apps/app/src/components/circles/partner/*`, `apps/app/src/components/circles/my-membership-card.tsx`, `apps/app/src/app/partner/index.tsx`; backend `apps/api/src/circles/circle-signals.*` (`docs/CIRCLES.md`).
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23 (updated 2026-11-24 for D32).
- **Scripture in the UI:** none typed. The evidence card has two i18n topic labels that link into the hadith reader (`/hadith/muslim?q=<corpus id>`); the reader shows the corpus text. One English paraphrase line (`partnerCircle.evidence.intro`) describes the hadith's teaching; it is a description, not a translation.

## How the references were checked

1. **Corpus:** the app's remote hadith corpus `fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1` (`apps/app/src/api/hadith-remote.ts`): `editions/eng-muslim.min.json` (full) and `editions/ara-muslim/{6927,6929}.json`, fetched 2026-11-24.
2. **Numbering (the Muslim pitfall):** the plan cites **Sahih Muslim 2733** in Abd al-Baqi (print / sunnah.com) numbering. The corpus `hadithnumber` is sequential and different. The corpus `arabicnumber` field was searched for 2732–2733 in Book 48 ("The Book Pertaining to the Remembrance of Allah, Supplication, Repentance and Seeking Forgiveness", corpus 6805–6936):

**Finding:** there is no `arabicnumber` 2733.xx in the corpus. The Safwan narration, which Abd al-Baqi numbers 2733, is labelled `2732.03` in the corpus (its `arabicnumber` collapses 2732 and 2733). It was identified by its text («دعوة المرء المسلم لأخيه بظهر الغيب مستجابة…»), which is the wording known as Muslim 2733. The in-app link therefore uses **`muslim:6930`** for "Muslim 2733" and **`muslim:6927`** for its companion narration (Abd al-Baqi 2732). The corpus English for 6927 says "Abu Dharr reported"; the Arabic isnad is Umm al-Darda' → Abu al-Darda' — an English-edition error, recorded here; the reader shows both texts.
