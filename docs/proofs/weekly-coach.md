# Proof log: Weekly gentle coach (NF-3.14, D3/D11)

- **Feature:** once a week, 1–3 kind, actionable insights from the user's own tracking, plus one micro-goal they can accept (`/coach`, home module `weekly-coach`, weekly notification "Your weekly reflection is ready").
- **Code:** engine `packages/shared/src/utils/coach/` (`rules.ts`, `engine.ts`, `history.ts`, `week.ts`; tests `coach.test.ts`, fixtures `packages/shared/src/test-support/coach.ts`); types `packages/shared/src/types/coach.ts`; app context builder `apps/app/src/lib/coach/context.ts` (+ `load-context.ts`), UI mapping `lib/coach/coach-ui.ts`, reminder `lib/coach/reminders.ts`; copy `apps/app/scripts/i18n/weekly-coach-i18n.json` (`coach.*`).
- **D3:** deterministic rules + i18n templates, computed on the device. No LLM, no network, no server inference. Vault features (muhasabah, family ties) reach the coach only as counts through `readMuhasabahWeekSummary` / `readFamilyTiesSummary` (null when the vault can't be opened — those rules are skipped).
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-24. The coach makes no new fiqh rulings. Its copy is encouragement and procedure; where it touches a ruling (travel, illness, hayd) it links to the madhhab-aware guides (`/travel`, `/sick`, `/hayd`) instead of stating a school's position.
- **Rendering rule:** no Qur'an or hadith text is typed. Each insight may carry one corpus id (`COACH_EVIDENCE` in `rules.ts`) rendered as a link into the hadith collection. Labels (`coach.evidence.*`) are short descriptions, not quotations.
- **UI disclaimer:** "For questions about your own situation, consult a qualified scholar." (`coach.disclaimer`).

## 2. Claims in the copy

1. **"Your streak is safe" in an excused week.** App rule, not a fiqh claim: excused days are transparent to streaks (`computeStreak` / `longestStreak`, NF-1.2). A broken streak never takes back the personal best (`longestStreak`).
2. **Illness: "pray as you are able"; "the reward of what you used to do when well continues, in sha Allah".** Bukhari 1117 and Bukhari 2996. The app's "sick" excuse pauses tracking only — the copy deliberately does not say salah is lifted. "In sha Allah" keeps the reward a hope, not a guarantee.
3. **Travel: "the reward of what you used to do at home continues"; "your school's view on shortening salah is in the travel guide".** Bukhari 2996. Qasr status and distance differ between the schools (`qasr.status`, `qasr.distance` in `FIQH_DIFFERENCES`, `madhhab.md`), so the coach links to `/travel`, which shows the user's school / all views, instead of stating one.
4. **Hayd / nifas: "zikr and dua stay open to you".** Agreed: remembrance of Allah (tasbih, tahlil, dua) is permitted for a woman in hayd or nifas — al-Nawawi reports consensus in _al-Majmu' Sharh al-Muhadhdhab_ (Kitab al-Hayd, the rulings of the ha'id). **Qur'an recitation is deliberately not mentioned** because the schools differ (`hayd.quranRecitation`, `madhhab.md`); the hayd guide link shows that difference.
5. **Qaza: "make them up gently, and ask Allah's forgiveness for what was missed".** Gentle-tone wording from plan §2. The rule only fires for users who keep a qaza tracker themselves; it states no ruling on the obligation of making up deliberately missed salah (where scholars differ). Bukhari 597 ("whoever forgets a salah, let him pray it when he remembers") was checked (`bukhari:597`) but is not shown, because it addresses forgetting, not every case the tracker holds.
6. **Fajr: "going to sleep a little earlier and reciting the before-sleep adhkar can help".** Practical advice; Bukhari 568 is the supporting sunnah. The before-sleep adhkar text comes from the bundled adhkar dataset (not typed here).
7. **Rawatib: "a lovely next step is the two rakahs before Fajr".** Muslim 725 (its virtue). The schools grade and name it differently (e.g. the Maliki school calls it _raghibah_; the Shafi'i school ranks Witr above it), so the copy neither grades nor ranks it — "scholars differ" on the term, and no school's position is imposed. Daily set sizes (10 / 12) follow D8 on the rawatib screen.
8. **Congregation: "could one salah this week be with the congregation at your masjid?".** Bukhari 645 (virtue). The ruling on congregational salah differs (e.g. Hanbali: obligatory on men; Shafi'i: communal obligation; Hanafi/Maliki: emphasised sunnah). The copy is an invitation with no ruling. Per the plan, the rule **never infers gender**: it only fires for users who set a home masjid themselves.
9. **Khushu: "learning the meaning of what you recite can help the heart be present".** Practical advice; links to the salah phrases guide (`/salah-guide/phrases`), whose content has its own proof entries.
10. **"Alhamdulillah", "May Allah accept them", "May Allah make it a light for you".** General duas and praise; no claim.
11. **Focus lock (`focus_emergency`, added 2026-11-24): "Emergency unlock was used {{done}} times this week. That is simply a count, and some days need it…".** NF-3.13 §5 says emergency unlocks reach the coach "only as a neutral count". The rule reads the device-local unlock log (`focusLockStore.emergencyLog`, never synced) through `emergencyCountLastWeek`, counting the 7 days of the reviewed week. It fires only from 3 unlocks, only for users who set focus lock up on this device, at most once every 2 weeks, never in an excused week, and with no goal and no evidence ref. The copy is an invitation to adjust the windows or unlock mode; it states no ruling and makes no judgement about the user.

## 3. Tone and privacy checks (enforced by tests)

- `apps/app/src/lib/coach/coach-copy.test.ts`: every `coach.*` string is free of `%`, "fail", "lost", "broke", "should have", "bad", comparisons with other people ("others", "everyone", "most people"…). Numbers are shown as counts ("4 of 7").
- At most one suggestion per area; ≤ 3 insights; ≥ 1 celebration while anything was tracked (`packages/shared/src/utils/coach/coach.test.ts`).
- Excused weeks (≥ 4 excused days or an active period) show only the rest message — no suggestion, no goal check, no new goal — and the weekly notification is paused.
- The notification body is always "Your weekly reflection is ready" (no counts, no vault data). Sentry: tags only (`area: coach`).
- `coach_history` stores rule ids, goals and outcomes, dismiss/snooze — no raw data.
