# Proof log: Hands-free tasbeeh (NF-3.20, D3 / D16 / D18 / D23 / D24 / D48–D51)

- **Feature:** the tasbeeh counter counts a catalog zikr when it is said out loud, using the pinned FastConformer CTC model **on the device** (never OS or cloud speech recognition; audio never leaves the device, D3). Every recognised phrase goes through the counter shell's single `countOnce()` path, so every NF-3.19 design, haptic and the target limit respond exactly as to a tap.
- **Shipped mode (D24, see §4 Decision):** the phrase spotter missed the spike gate, so v1 ships the documented **pause-count fallback** — each separate spoken burst counts once, labelled "Counts each pause — less accurate". The model-based spotter is built and tested but switched off (`lib/tasbeeh-voice/mode.ts`).
- **Teach a zikr (D48–D51, see §7):** any zikr can be taught by saying it 1–3 times; the counter then counts only repeats of it (query-by-example matching of on-device voice features, no model, no download), labelled "Counts only the zikr you taught — tap to correct any miscount". Untaught zikr, or "Skip — count every pause", keep the pause-count fallback. Ships without the gate (D51); measured in §7.
- **Code:** `apps/app/src/lib/speech/` (shared FastConformer runner), `apps/app/src/lib/tasbeeh-voice/*` (catalog, VAD, spotter, de-dupe, gate, session, pipeline; teach-a-zikr: `features.ts`, `dtw.ts`, `matcher.ts`, `template-count.ts`, `template-store.ts`, `voice-zikr.ts`), `apps/app/src/stores/tasbeeh-voice-templates-store.ts`, `apps/app/src/hooks/use-voice-tasbeeh.ts`, `apps/app/src/components/tasbeeh/voice/*`, `apps/app/src/components/tasbeeh/tasbeeh-counter.tsx`; build/eval scripts `apps/app/scripts/build-tasbeeh-voice-tokens.mjs`, `apps/app/scripts/tasbeeh-voice-eval.mjs`, `apps/app/scripts/tasbeeh-voice-template-eval.mjs` (`--template`).
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23/24.
- **Scripture in the UI:** none typed. The zikr being listened for is shown **from the bundled adhkar / durood dataset entry** (`packages/shared/src/content/zikr.ts`, `duroods.ts`), with that entry's own translation; a custom tasbeeh shows the user's own title. Phrase _names_ in the UI ("SubhanAllah", "La ilaha illallah") are transliterated labels, not scripture.

## 3. Model / tokenisation facts (spike S1, measured 2026-11-23)

- **Files:** all five FastConformer files downloaded from the D18 mirror `huggingface.co/acibZ/tilawa-quran-onnx` @ `0cd79471524bc9cfa1c9296055242a935a1873e4` — sha256 of every file equals the pin in `constants/recitation-models.json` (88,307,366 B model). `webHosted: true` with per-file `webUrl` set; the web model store downloads from the mirror and applies the same SHA-256 check; `fetch-recitation-models.mjs` never copies files that have a `webUrl` (no self-hosting).
- **I/O:** `audio_signal` float32 [1, N] + `length` int64 [1] → `log_probs` [1, T, 1025]; **one frame ≈ 79.6–80 ms**; blank = 1024 (`export_metadata.json`). Preprocessing is inside the graph.
- **Vocab:** SentencePiece BPE (`▁` word marker). The "mixed" model **emits diacritised pieces** (e.g. `▁س|ُ|ب|ْ|ح|َ|ان|َ|▁الل|َّ|هِ`), while `quran_ctc_tokens.json` holds bare-text tokens; `tokenizer.model` is **not** part of the pin, and greedy/BPE-by-id re-tokenisation reproduced only ~3% of `quran_ctc_tokens.json`. **Decision:** the spotter does not depend on one segmentation — it searches a _lattice_ of every vocab piece that matches the folded phrase text, with pure-harakat pieces as fillers (`phrase-tokens.json`, 1,677 lattice edges, 12 filler pieces). Every catalog phrase is spellable from the pinned vocab (unit-tested).
- **Coverage:** every catalog word is spellable; whole-word pieces exist for e.g. `▁الله`, `▁سبحان`, `▁لله`, `▁بالله`, `▁اللهم`, `▁محمد`, `▁حول`, `▁العظيم`.
- **Zipformer:** its ONNX output **is CTC** (`log_probs` over 251 phoneme tokens, streaming, fbank features in JS). It is used here only for the D23 cross-check (phoneme transcripts vs the Zipformer Qur'an phoneme corpus). Counting with Zipformer would need a phoneme lexicon for non-Qur'anic words — **not built in v1**; a user with only Zipformer installed is offered the FastConformer download (Qur'an practice keeps one listener; FastConformer also works for practice, follow-along only).
- **Spotter comparison:** see §4 — the CTC lattice spotter beat greedy decode + fuzzy match, but neither met the gate.
- **Scoring fix found by the spike:** normalising the path LLR per _frame_ let a long run of blanks dilute a forced path until silence matched; the score is now per emitted piece (unit-tested: "never counts silence").

## 4. Spike S2 — fixture evaluation (D23)

**All numbers are "measured on reference + synthetic audio".** They are not a claim about real learners' voices, and the app copy never makes one.

- **No new recordings, no storage of our own.** `pnpm --filter app tasbeeh-voice:eval` builds the set at eval time from audio the app already streams: the Hisnul Muslim adhkar/dua recordings behind each item's `audioUri` (`lib/audio-tracks.ts`) and everyayah ayah recitations (Alafasy, Husary, Minshawi, Abdul Basit). Cached in `node_modules/.cache/munib-tasbeeh-voice-eval/` (git-ignored); never committed.
- **Augmentation (ffmpeg):** speed 0.8/1.2/1.4×, pitch ±3 semitones, −24 dB low volume, whisper-like band-limit (400–3200 Hz, −14 dB), room reverb, fan (pink) / car (brown) / crowd (reversed multi-reciter babble) noise at 10–12 dB SNR. Phrase snippets (cut by forced alignment from cross-checked reference clips) are spliced 5× with 600 ms gaps (normal pace) and 5× back-to-back at 1.3× (fast pace).
- **Negatives:** Qur'an recitation, every other catalog phrase (each clip is scored for all 8 phrases, so SubhanAllah audio is a negative for Alhamdulillah, etc.), duas that contain no catalog phrase, 20 s noise beds and 10 s silence.
- **Truth** comes from each clip's dataset / Qur'an text (folded, alif-insensitive so Uthmani and imla'i spellings agree).
- **Cross-check:** a reference clip is used only if the FastConformer greedy transcript (normalised distance ≤ 0.30) **and** the Zipformer phoneme transcript (where the Zipformer corpus covers ≥ 50% of the words) agree with the text. Excluded clips are listed below.
- Unit tests use only synthetic log-prob matrices (`test-support/tasbeeh-voice.ts`).

### Decision (D24)

**Spike S2 missed the release gate** at every threshold: over-count ≥ 0.84% (gate ≤ 0.5%) and missed counts 14% normal / 30% fast at the strictest point (gate ≤ 5% / ≤ 10%). Per D24 the **documented fallback ships without asking again: "Pause count (basic)"** — every separate spoken burst counts once, no model and no download, labelled in the sheet as "Counts each pause — less accurate". `lib/tasbeeh-voice/mode.ts` sets `VOICE_COUNTING_MODE = "pause"`; the model-based spotter, calibration, sensitivity and model-storage settings are present but switched off (hidden) until a re-run of `pnpm --filter app tasbeeh-voice:eval` passes, at which point the constant flips back to `"spotter"`.

Follow-ups that could lift the spotter over the gate: listen-verify the over-counted Hisnul recordings (label noise), add the D23 word-by-word (audio.qurancdn.com) splices for `istighfar` / `salawat_short`, per-phrase thresholds, and phone measurements.

## 6. Privacy

- Capture: `lib/quran-companion/audio/capture.*` (expo-audio stream / getUserMedia), 16 kHz mono, nothing written to disk. Silence never reaches the model (energy VAD).
- Stored: `tasbeehVoice` prefs (sensitivity, auto-stop seconds; synced with preferences), `DB_KEYS.tasbeehVoiceCalibration` (one offset per phrase) and `DB_KEYS.tasbeehVoiceTemplates` (D48: per taught zikr, 1–3 quantised feature matrices + a threshold + when it was taught; at most 20 zikr). Both are `deviceLocal`: not synced, not backed up, not in the vault, wiped on reset; the templates are also wiped by Settings › Tasbeeh counter › "Forget taught zikr voices", and replaced by "Re-teach".
- Never stored or sent: audio, transcripts, phrase text, scores. A "Something else" pattern (no zikr to name it by) lives only in memory for the session. Sentry: error codes only (`area: quran-practice`, `phase: tasbeeh-voice-*`).
- Microphone strings (`app.json`, iOS + Android) now mention hands-free tasbeeh counting; audio stays on the device. Play Data safety stays "audio not collected".

## 7. Teach a zikr — query-by-example matching (D48–D51)

**What ships.** Any zikr (D49): a catalog zikr, durood or custom tasbeeh counter listens for its own zikr; the free counter has a searchable picker (whole adhkar library + the user's tasbeehs + "Something else"). The sheet's first step (D50) asks the person to say the zikr up to 3 times ("Got it 1/3", level meter); each spoken burst becomes a template. While counting, the energy VAD cuts the audio into bursts and each burst is searched for the templates; every non-overlapping match is one count through the shell's `countOnce()`. "Skip — count every pause" keeps the D24 pause count. No model, no download, nothing leaves the device.

**Method** (`lib/tasbeeh-voice/`, pure TS, unit-tested on synthetic signals only):

- `features.ts`: 25 ms frames every 10 ms → pre-emphasis, Hamming, 512-point FFT → 26 mel bands → 13 MFCCs + deltas, per-utterance mean/variance normalisation. A steady background is estimated per band from the quietest 10% of frames (only when they are ≥ 10 dB below the median frame, i.e. a real pause) and subtracted; mel valleys deeper than −25 dB below the loudest band are floored. The floor was the change that made fan / car / crowd noise match (fan-noise misses 100% → 3% at normal pace in the quick runs).
- `dtw.ts`: path-length-normalised DTW (template vs template) and subsequence DTW (template inside a burst, free start / end, speed 0.6–1.7×); matches are picked best-first without overlap, so two fast repeats in one burst count twice.
- `matcher.ts`: threshold = clamp(spread × 1.1 + 0.06, 0.6, 0.6) — the calibrated range currently pins it to an average frame cost of **0.6** (single sample: 0.6). Energy sanity check: a match quieter than the burst's peak by more than 22 dB (echo, tail) does not count. Teaching rejects a sample < 300 ms, > 8 s, or > 0.75 away from an earlier sample ("That sounded different").
- `template-count.ts`: streaming — bursts are searched at speech-end; during unbroken speech the open burst is searched every ~1 s and matches ending > 450 ms before the live edge count at once.

### Evaluation (`pnpm --filter app tasbeeh-voice:eval --template`, 2026-11-24)

**All numbers are "measured on reference + synthetic audio"** — never a claim about real voices.

- **Set:** the 15 phrase snippets the spotter eval cuts and cross-checks (§4: SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, La ilaha illallah × 3 recordings each, SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi × 2, Hawqala × 1; none survived for Istighfar / Salawat). Cached by `tasbeeh-voice-eval.mjs --snippets-only`; the template eval runs no model.
- **"The person" = one snippet.** We have one recording per voice, so the three teaching takes are that snippet at 0.92× and 1.08× tempo and +1 semitone, each padded with 300 ms of quiet like a VAD burst — none of them is a test augmentation.
- **Positives (same voice):** 5 repeats with 600 ms pauses (normal pace) under 12 augmentations (clean, speed 0.8/1.2/1.4×, pitch ±3 semitones, −24 dB, whisper-like band-limit, reverb, fan / car / crowd noise at 10–12 dB SNR), and 5 repeats back-to-back at 1.3× (fast pace) under the 9 non-speed augmentations. 15 enrolments, 553 clips, 1,725 VAD bursts.
- **Negatives:** every other catalog phrase's snippets (5× sequences, all augmentations, ~10 h of audio across enrolments), 217 natural Qur'an / adhkar / dua recordings that do not contain the phrase (other speech, ~10.5 h across enrolments), the taught snippet played backwards (same sounds, wrong order), synthetic coughs (2 levels × 10), fan / car / crowd noise beds and 10 s of silence. 126 (enrolment × snippet) pairs were skipped because the other phrase's snippet was cut from the same recording as the teacher's (e.g. "SubhanAllah" and "SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi" from one clip — overlapping audio, ambiguous label).
- **Metric:** over-count = every extra count (on positives and negatives) per positive phrase presented, as in §4.

**Where misses and extra counts come from (0.6, three samples):**

- Pitch ±3 semitones (asetrate) moves the formants ~19%, which is closer to a different voice than to the same person repeating: 73–100% missed. Every other same-voice condition at normal pace: clean, speed 0.8–1.4×, low volume, whisper-like and reverb 0%; fan 2.7%, car 2.7%, crowd 1.3%. Fast pace: clean 2.7%, low volume 2.7%, whisper-like 9.3%, fan / car 9.3%, reverb / crowd 10.7%.
- **Other voices** saying the same zikr (cross-recording) are missed 91.8% of the time, and the zikr inside longer natural recordings 89.6%: the matcher is speaker- and delivery-specific by design (it counts the voice that taught it, so another person talking nearby normally doesn't count).
- 3 extra counts in ~20 h of negative audio: Allahu Akbar taught from two recordings matched once each inside Qur'an 37:159 (Alafasy), and SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi matched once in a SubhanAllah sequence with car noise. Reversed speech, coughs, noise beds and silence: 0.

**Streaming pipeline** (the app's `createTemplateCounter`, 100 ms chunks, every enrolment: normal clean, fast clean, normal fan noise + 4 other-phrase sequences): 221 counted for 225 said (1.8% missed), **0 extra counts** (0 counts in 9.5 min of other-phrase sequences) — the open-burst search counts fast repeats without double counting.
