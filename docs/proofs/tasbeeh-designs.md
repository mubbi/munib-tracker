# Proof log: Tasbeeh counter designs (NF-3.19, D10 / D16 / D28)

- **Feature:** choosable tasbeeh counter designs (Ring default, sliding-bead misbaha, digital clicker, Focus / Night; v2: Date pits / pebbles, Finger counting, Geometric rosette, Night sky), CC0 click sounds and haptic levels.
- **Code:** `apps/app/src/lib/tasbeeh/*`, `apps/app/src/components/tasbeeh/faces/*`, `apps/app/src/components/tasbeeh/tasbeeh-counter.tsx`, `tasbeeh-appearance-editor.tsx`, `tasbeeh-style-sheet.tsx`; sounds in `apps/app/assets/audio/tasbeeh/` (README lists every file).
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23.
- **Scripture in the UI:** none. No hadith or Qur'an text is typed or shown by this feature; the claims below govern design decisions and the one copy line about sound ("No music, no bells").

## How the numbers were checked

Two corpora were used, both the app's own sources:

1. **Bundled:** `apps/app/assets/data/hadith/riyad-as-salihin.json` (searched by Arabic text and English keywords).
2. **Remote (cache-first in the app):** `fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1` — the CDN `apps/app/src/api/hadith-remote.ts` loads (`HADITH_CDN = https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions`). Files read: `editions/{eng,ara}-abudawud/{1500,1501}.json`, `editions/{eng,ara}-tirmidhi/{3568,3583}.json`, `editions/{eng,ara}-muslim/2114.json` and the full `editions/eng-muslim.min.json` (searched by `arabicnumber`). `ara-abudawud/1501.json` exceeds jsDelivr's package limit, so it was read from `raw.githubusercontent.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api/1/editions/ara-abudawud/1501.json` (the same fallback `static-json-fetch.ts` uses).

**Sahih Muslim numbering pitfall.** The plan's "Muslim 2114" is the Fuad Abd al-Baqi number. In the app corpus `muslim:2114` (`hadithnumber` 2114, `arabicnumber` 911.01) is an unrelated hadith (the eclipse hadith of Abu Mas'ud). The bell hadith is **`muslim:5548`** (`arabicnumber` 2114, Book 37, in-book 159). Any in-app link must use `muslim:5548`. Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi numbers match (`hadithnumber` = `arabicnumber`).

Copy for the Fingers design must cite **Abu Dawud 1501 (hasan)** as the primary reference and present Tirmidhi 3583 with its differing gradings. The bundled Riyad as-Salihin file does not contain this hadith (searched for «الأنامل» / "fingertips" in a counting context — no match).

## 2. Claim: using date pits or pebbles — reports graded differently

Used for: the **Date pits / pebbles** design (v2, shipped 2026-11-24 with the always-visible grading note per D16) and the general position that beads are permissible.

| Ref (plan)                     | Corpus id             | What was checked                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Gradings recorded in the corpus                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Tirmidhi 3568                  | `tirmidhi:3568`       | Book 48, in-book 199. Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas entered with the Prophet ﷺ upon a woman «وَبَيْنَ يَدَيْهَا نَوًى أَوْ قَالَ حَصًى تُسَبِّحُ بِهِ», and he taught her a fuller tasbih ("…as many as what He created in the heaven…"). | Abu Dawud 1500                 | `abudawud:1500`       | Book 8, in-book 85. Same report via the same chain (Khuzaymah ← 'A'ishah bint Sa'd ← Sa'd). Number and text match.                                                                                                                                            | Al-Albani: **Da'if**; Muhyi al-Din Abd al-Hamid: Da'if; Shu'aib al-Arna'ut: **Hasan li-ghayrihi**; Zubair Ali Zai: isnad hasan |
| (bundled) Riyad as-Salihin 763 | `riyad_assalihin:763` | The same Sa'd report, ending «رواه الترمذي وقال: حديث حسن». **Ruling recorded:** _scholars differ_ on the grading. The report does not forbid counting with date pits or pebbles (the Prophet ﷺ is not reported as rejecting them; he taught a fuller wording). Most scholars allow beads, pebbles and date pits; several hold fingers are better (attributed in later fiqh works, e.g. Ibn Taymiyyah's _Majmu' al-Fatawa_, vol. 22 — **page not verified in this review**, so no app copy asserts it; any copy says "scholars differ"). The Pebbles design must show this grading note and link both refs.

## 5. Copy and design guardrails as implemented

- **No reward claims:** no design name, description or note says or implies more reward (`tasbeehDesigns.designs.*.desc` — checked in all 24 locales' source English).
- **No living beings:** faces are a ring, beads, an LCD device, numerals, dishes of date pits / pebbles, a geometric rosette and a night sky (stars and a crescent). The Finger counting design draws a hand as an outline only (theme ink on the card colour, no skin tones, no face or figure) — a body part used as a counting diagram, as the plan specifies.
- **No music:** 24 CC0 percussive clips only; no `impactBell_*`, chime, `confirmation`, `pluck`, `bong` or tonal clips. Sound is off by default on phones, on by default on TV (D16).
- **Calm, not game-like:** no points, unlocks or paywalls; every design and material is free (D16). Ring keeps its confetti; the other designs use a static glow.
- **Accuracy:** a design never changes the count (shell owns counting; tests: one tap = one count, limit holds, a drag counts at most once and never subtracts, 1,000 rapid taps = 1,000 counts per face).

- **No text typed:** the notes paraphrase; no hadith wording (Arabic or English) is reproduced. The Ibn Taymiyyah attribution (§2) stays out of the copy — "others hold that counting on the fingertips is better" is the only wording, in the "scholars differ" form.

- **No gamification:** rounds show "Round N · x of y" only; no stars collected, no streaks or rewards; completion is a static glow (no confetti) on every v2 design.
- **TV:** none of the v2 designs is offered on TV yet (plan: phone and web only until TV QA); a synced choice shows Ring on TV without being rewritten.
