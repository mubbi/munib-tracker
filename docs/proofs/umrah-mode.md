# Proof log: Umrah mode, live guided (NF-3.4, D11)

- **Feature:** `/umrah`, a step-by-step companion during Umrah. Steps: prepare → miqat → ihram → talbiyah → arrive at the Haram → tawaf (7) → two rakahs behind Maqam Ibrahim → Zamzam → sa'i (7) → halq / taqsir → complete. It has a miqat check (by land: foreground GPS ≤ 20 km; by air: a manual crossing-time countdown), tawaf and sa'i counters with undo and an optional per-lap timer, duas rendered by reference, the `tawaf.wudu` madhhab note, a hayd / nifas lock and a home "Continue your Umrah" card. It extends the `umrah_checklist` store and does not replace it.
- **Code:**
  - types: `packages/shared/src/types/umrah.ts`
  - state machine, locks, miqat distance, sync merge: `packages/shared/src/utils/umrah-session.ts`
  - step content (ids and refs only): `packages/shared/src/content/umrah-mode.ts`
  - store: `apps/app/src/stores/umrah-session-store.ts` (blob entity `umrah_sessions`)
  - UI: `apps/app/src/app/umrah/index.tsx`, `apps/app/src/components/umrah/*`
  - miqat data: `apps/app/scripts/build-data/build-miqat.mjs` → `apps/app/assets/data/miqat.json`
  - copy: `apps/app/scripts/i18n/umrah-mode-i18n.json` (24 locales)
- **Guide prose is not duplicated.** Every step links its existing `hajj-guide.ts` topic (`/hajj/[topic]`). The step copy is short procedural guidance under `umrahMode.*`.
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23.
- **UI disclaimer:** `hajj.disclaimer` ("A practical guide, not a substitute for your official Hajj/Umrah provider").

## 0. How the refs were checked

- **Qur'an:** against the bundled corpus, `apps/app/assets/data/quran/arabic/002.json` and `translation/en-pickthall/002.json`.
- **Hadith:** against fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1, the corpus the app renders (`eng-<collection>.min.json`, fetched 2026-11-24).
  - **Numbering gotcha:** Muslim refs were paired by the corpus `arabicnumber` field (Abd al-Baqi). Both numbers are recorded below. The app stores the corpus id with `arabicNumber`, and `EvidenceLine` prints the Abd al-Baqi number.
  - Bukhari, Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi corpus numbers equal their printed numbers for every ref below (checked: `hadithnumber == arabicnumber`).
- **Duas:** shown only by reference from the bundled Hisnul Muslim dataset (`packages/shared/src/content/duas.ts`). The dataset is generated by `build-adhkar.mjs` and never edited. `DuaByRef` looks up the id and renders the shared `ReadingCard`: Arabic, transliteration, the user's translation locale, reference, and recorded audio (jsDelivr, sheikhhanif/Hisnul_Muslim_Database).
  - `umrah-mode.test.ts` asserts that every referenced id exists.
  - No Qur'an, hadith or dua text is typed in code or i18n.

## 2. Rulings and app choices

### Hayd and nifas: the tawaf lock (`umrahStepLockReason`)

- **Claim (lock copy):** tawaf waits until the hayd or nifas ends and ghusl is made, and the ihram stays.
  - **Bukhari 305** (`bukhari:305`): 'A'ishah got her menses at Sarif. The Prophet ﷺ said: "…do what all the pilgrims do with the exception of the Tawaf around the Ka'ba till you are clean."
  - **Bukhari 1650** (`bukhari:1650`): "Perform all the ceremonies of Hajj like the other pilgrims, but do not perform Tawaf of the Ka'ba till you get clean." Here 'A'ishah also reports she did not perform sa'i.
  - **Per school:** all four schools say a menstruating woman does not perform tawaf. The Hanafis hold that a tawaf done anyway is valid, with a penalty. That nuance is the same one `tawaf.wudu` shows for the Hanafi school ("wajib, valid without it, with a penalty"), and that note is on the same step. The plan's wording "tawaf requires purity per the majority" is kept in spirit. The copy states the instruction all four agree on ("waits until you are pure"), not a validity ruling.
- **Two rakahs:** locked during hayd and nifas. No salah is prayed during hayd (consensus; `hayd.*` guide). Wording: "wait until you are pure".
- **Sa'i:**
  - Locked while tawaf is not complete: in Umrah, sa'i follows tawaf (Bukhari 1650 above; Muslim 1218 order).
  - **Not locked once tawaf is done.** Copy (`umrahMode.sai.purityNote`): "Wudu is recommended for sa'i, but it is not a condition of sa'i in the relied-upon positions of the four schools."
  - Source: al-Mughni (Ibn Qudamah), _Kitab al-Hajj_, on sa'i. Purity is not a condition of sa'i for most scholars. A narration from Ahmad requires it, but it is not the relied-upon Hanbali position (Zad al-Mustaqni' lists purity among the sunnahs of sa'i). The note says "relied-upon positions", so that narration is not denied.
  - This was not added as a `FIQH_DIFFERENCES` row, because the four schools' relied-upon positions agree.
- **nifas** is treated like hayd for these locks (the same ruling for tawaf and salah).
- **"Wait" guidance:** the copy is "Stay in ihram… when you are pure, make ghusl and continue… If your group must leave before then, ask a scholar about your situation."
  - Necessity cases, such as a departing flight, are deliberately not ruled on: the scholars differ (Ibn Taymiyyah's necessity view, and the Hanafi dam).
  - The step links the existing `/hayd` guide.
- Other excused reasons (sick, travel) lock nothing: a traveller is exactly who performs Umrah.

### Doubt about the number of circuits

- **Copy:** "If you are unsure, build on the lesser number, the one you are certain of, and complete the rest. A doubt that comes after you have finished is not acted on."
- **Sources:**
  - **Muslim 571** (`muslim:1272`, 571.01; sahih): Abu Sa'id: "When any one of you is in doubt about his prayer… he should cast aside his doubt and base his prayer on what he is sure of." This is the base rule the scholars apply to tawaf by analogy.
  - **al-Mughni** (Ibn Qudamah), _Kitab al-Hajj_, on tawaf. Ibn al-Mundhir reports consensus that whoever doubts in his tawaf builds on certainty. A doubt after finishing tawaf is not considered.
- **Madhhab-neutral:** no school is named. The plan asked for a "scholar check"; under D11 this proof replaces it.

### Halq / taqsir

- Men choose halq (preferred) or taqsir; the choice is stored on the session as `hairChoice`. Women shorten by about a fingertip's length and do not shave (Abu Dawud 1984; al-Mughni).
- How much of the head a man must cut for taqsir **differs between the schools**: Hanafi, at least a quarter of the head; Shafi'i, at least three hairs; Maliki and Hanbali, the whole head. Since 2026-11-24 this is the `FIQH_DIFFERENCES` row `umrah.taqsir` (guide note only). It is rendered as a `MadhhabNote` on the halq / taqsir step (`SCREEN_DIFFERENCES["/umrah#halq_taqsir"]`) and on the Hajj guide topic "Halq or taqsir". Sources for each school: `madhhab.md` §10. The step's own copy still states no amount.

### Zamzam

- **Copy:** "a recommended practice, not one of the rites of Umrah."
- **Evidence:** the Prophet ﷺ drank Zamzam during his Hajj (Muslim 1218 / `muslim:2951`, the Zamzam passage).
- The popular "Zamzam is for whatever it is drunk for" (Ibn Majah 3062) is **not cited**: its gradings differ, and the existing guide already words it cautiously.

- **OSM:** none of these items carries an OSM id (P402). Wikidata alone is used, which the plan allows ("OSM/Wikidata by id").
- **Radius:** ≤ 20 km means "Approaching miqat" (plan). Distance is great-circle (haversine).
- **Wording:** the copy says "about N km" and always adds "Be in ihram before you pass it". The al-Juhfah point is the historic site; most pilgrims today use the Rabigh area, so the label reads "Al-Juhfah (Rabigh)".
- **By air:** the user types the crossing time, and the app shows an in-app countdown. The hint notes that GPS is unreliable in flight and many airlines announce the miqat.
- **Privacy:** the foreground location is read on the device only, while the miqat step is open (a quiet re-check every 2 minutes, only when permission is already granted). It is never stored or sent. No notification is scheduled.

## 3. Privacy, excused periods, notifications

- **Session data:** steps, counts, lap times, travel mode, flight time and hair choice. It is synced as the normal blob `umrah_sessions` (not sensitive under D2), with per-session LWW and tombstones on "End Umrah mode".
- **No hayd state is stored on the session:** the lock reads `activeExcusedReason` live.
- **No notifications** (so no budget entry or `notificationPrefs` toggle is needed). The flight timer is in-app only.
