# Proof log: Family & kids mode (NF-3.16, D7 / D27 / D34)

- **Feature:** a family circle (owner = parent, co-parent role `parent`, young people 13+ as `member` with their own account, children under 13 as parent-managed `child` profiles holding a nickname and birth year only), parental consent recorded with a policy version, a one-time QR to link the child's device to a scoped child session, kids mode (Pray / Learn / Duas), stars and stickers, a parent-defined real reward, rest days, graduation to an own account, and a parent view that shows the five fard statuses only.
- **Code:** `apps/app/src/lib/circles/{api,hooks,family,kids-content,child-session,consent,inbox}.ts`, `apps/app/src/components/circles/*`, routes `apps/app/src/app/{circles,circle/join,family,kids}/*`, settings `apps/app/src/lib/settings/defs/circles.ts`. Backend: `apps/api/src/circles/*`, `docs/CIRCLES.md`.
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23 (references re-fetched 2026-11-24).
- **Scripture in the UI:** none typed. Evidence rows are i18n labels linking to the Qur'an reader. Kids-mode duas and adhkar are rendered from the bundled datasets by id (`@munib-tracker/shared/content/{duas,zikr}`); short surahs open the normal reader.

## How the references were checked

1. **Qur'an:** bundled Uthmani Arabic + Pickthall, `apps/app/assets/data/quran/{arabic,translation/en-pickthall}/<surah>.json` (keys are ayah numbers).
2. **Hadith:** the app's remote corpus `fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1` (`cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@1/editions/{eng,ara}-<collection>/<n>.json`, and the full `eng-muslim.min.json` to pair Sahih Muslim numbers). Bukhari, Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi corpus numbers equal the sunnah.com numbers (`hadithnumber == arabicnumber` for every ref below). **Sahih Muslim** is cited in Abd al-Baqi numbering; the corpus id is recorded next to it (paired by the corpus `arabicnumber` field).
3. **Gradings** are copied from the corpus `grades` array (Al-Albani, Muhammad Muhyi al-Din Abdul Hamid, Shu'aib al-Arna'ut, Zubair Ali Zai where present). Bukhari and Muslim are sahih by consensus.
4. **Dataset coverage** of the kids content (ids exist, audio exists, reference present) is enforced by `apps/app/src/lib/circles/kids-content.test.ts`.

## 3. Claim: graduation is offered at about 13 and again at 15; maturity (bulugh) is the real marker

- **Physical signs first.** All four schools treat bulugh as reached by its physical signs whenever they appear; an age is only a presumption when no sign has appeared. The app therefore lets the parent unlock graduation early (`graduationUnlocked`) and words the copy as "Maturity (bulugh) is the real marker".
- **Age of presumption — scholars differ.** Shafi'i, Hanbali and the two companions of Abu Hanifa (Abu Yusuf, Muhammad) presume bulugh at 15 lunar years; Abu Hanifa: 18 for boys, 17 for girls; the Maliki school is commonly reported at 18 (17 also narrated). Sources: al-Nawawi, _al-Majmu'_ (Kitab al-Hajr); Ibn Qudamah, _al-Mughni_ (Kitab al-Hajr, bab bulugh); al-Marghinani, _al-Hidayah_ (Kitab al-Hajr, fasl fi hadd al-bulugh); Ibn Rushd, _Bidayat al-Mujtahid_ (Kitab al-Hajr). Evidence the majority cite:

| Ref          | Corpus id      | What was checked                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Grading |
| ------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------- |
| Bukhari 2664 | `bukhari:2664` | Ibn 'Umar: presented at Uhud aged fourteen and not permitted; at the Trench aged fifteen and permitted; 'Umar ibn 'Abd al-'Aziz: "this age (fifteen) is the limit between childhood and manhood". Number and text match. | Sahih   |

- **Why 13 in the app:** the 13 cut-off for kids mode and the first graduation offer is a **product/privacy decision (D7)**, not a fiqh ruling — no age claim about bulugh is made at 13. The re-prompt at 15 follows the majority presumption above. The copy never states an age of obligation.
- **Wiring (E13, 2026-11-24):** `graduationPromptStage()` / `shouldShowGraduationPrompt()` in `@munib-tracker/shared/circles` (`GRADUATION_REPROMPT_AGE = 15`). On the child device the offer appears from 13 (or when a parent unlocks it) with a "Not now" button; dismissing it hides it until 15, when a distinct re-prompt (`kids.graduate.reprompt`) appears once more; dismissing that hides it for good (the graduate route still works). The parent's child card switches to `family.graduation.reprompt` from 15. Both re-prompt strings say "Schools differ on the age of maturity (bulugh); its signs are the real marker" — no school is named as the rule and no age of obligation is stated.

## 4. Claim: no hayd UI for children; generic rest days only

Not a ruling — a privacy/tone decision (D7). The rest-day signal is `{rest: true}` with no reason field (server DTO forbids extra fields). Rest days are left out of the weekly total so a break never reads as a shortfall.
