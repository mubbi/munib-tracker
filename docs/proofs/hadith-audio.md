# Proof log: Hadith audio (NF-3.23, D11 / D12 / D19)

- **Feature:** listen to hadith through the shared mini-player, using licence-cleared **human** recordings only (D19). No device voice (TTS) in any language.
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23 (source search re-run 2026-11-24).
- **Outcome: hadith audio does not ship.** No recording source passed the D12 licence review. Following D19 and §13 item 1, **no audio UI, engine, builder, `audio-index.json`, settings or credits entry was built**. The hadith screens stay as they are: the play control in `apps/app/src/app/hadith/[collection].tsx` only appears when an item has `audioUri`, and no bundled or remote hadith item has one. No hadith surface uses `lib/tts.ts`. This was checked: no route under `app/hadith/` or `components/hadith` imports TTS, and `enableTts` is only passed from `app/adhkar-builder`.

## 1. Islamic basis (refs checked against the corpus)

**How the basis is applied here:** because a wrong or unattributable recording would put words in the Prophet's ﷺ mouth that the app cannot vouch for, the accuracy and licence gates apply to every recording. A recording whose provenance is unclear fails D12, and it also fails the accuracy gate, because the reciter and the edition read cannot be confirmed.

## 2. D12 review criteria used

A source **passes** only if all of these hold:

1. **The licence is granted by the rights holder.** That means the reciter or publisher, or someone who shows they act for them. An uploader's own licence tag on someone else's recording does not count.
2. **The licence allows redistribution and streaming in a free, source-available app distributed through the app stores and the web.** That means CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, a genuine public-domain status, or published written permission. Under D12 a licence that is unclear for this use is not used. That covers **NonCommercial (NC)** terms, because their reach over app-store distribution and the project's public marketing site is unclear. "Free to download", "for personal use", "no licence stated" and YouTube uploads fail.
3. **The Public Domain Mark (PDM 1.0) is not a licence.** It only asserts that a work is already free of copyright. It cannot make a recent recording by a living reciter public domain, so a PDM on such a recording means the licence is unclear (fail).
4. **It is a recording of the hadith text itself, segmented per hadith.** It must map to `collection:number` in the bundled numbering (`nawawi40:1…42`, `riyad_assalihin:1…1896`) without text matching. Lectures or sharh (commentary) fail, because they aren't a recording of the hadith and their content isn't reviewed.
5. **It is publicly streamable without login,** from a stable URL with CORS for web (no self-hosting).

## 3. Search performed

- **Internet Archive** advanced search (`advancedsearch.php`) with `mediatype:audio AND licenseurl:[* TO *]`, over `title`, `subject` and `description`. Terms: hadith, hadeeth, hadits, hadis, nawawi, nawawiyya, riyad, riyadh, riyadus, saliheen, salihin, arbaeen, arbain, bukhari, الأربعين, رياض, الحديث, النووية. That gave about 1,500 hits, from which about 160 relevant items were triaged and the strongest candidates' metadata read through `archive.org/metadata/<id>`.
- **LibriVox** catalogue API (titles: hadith, muhammad, nawawi, bukhari, sayings, and others): no hadith recordings. LibriVox records only US-public-domain texts, and no hadith collection has been recorded.
- **Wikimedia Commons:** the API and site could not be reached from the review environment (DNS blocked). A web search of Commons found only Qur'an recitations and word-pronunciation clips, with no hadith recordings. _Re-check this from an unrestricted network._
- **Hugging Face** datasets (hadith/Islamic speech): `abdkm/hufazalmatoun`, `naxalpha/islamic-audios-v2`.
- **Publisher sites:** IslamHouse / islamcontent.com, HadeethEnc.com, House of Islam developer API, Kalamullah, abdurrahman.org, Muslim Central-style podcast hosts, and app-store "Riyad as-Salihin audio" apps.

## 5. Result

**No source passes D12.** Under D19 and §13 item 1: **hadith audio does not ship.** This is an accepted outcome (NF-3.23 risks), not a blocker.

What was deliberately **not** built: `apps/app/src/lib/hadith-audio.ts`, `scripts/build-data/build-hadith-audio.mjs`, `assets/data/hadith/audio-index.json`, play buttons, the "Has audio" filter, the source badge, `UserPreferences.hadithAudio`, the "Hadith, duas & adhkar › Hadith audio" settings, the service-worker host allowlist entry, the Credits/NOTICE entry, and i18n strings. No TTS fallback exists or was added.

## 6. Re-check criteria (when to reopen)

Reopen NF-3.23 only when a source meets **all** of the following, verified on the rights holder's own page:

1. A **named reciter or publisher** publishes, on their own site or channel (or an archive.org account demonstrably theirs), a licence of **CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA**, or written permission covering redistribution and streaming in a free app on the app stores and web. NC or ND terms need an explicit owner decision before they can count.
2. The audio is a **reading of the hadith text** (Arabic matn, or a translation identified by translator and edition), **one file per hadith**, numbered in a scheme that maps to the bundled corpus (`nawawi40:1–42`, `riyad_assalihin:1–1896`) or to the remote corpus ids (checked by `arabicnumber`, per the Muslim numbering gotcha). A translation recording must be of the **same translation the app displays**, or be shown with its own translator credit.
3. The files are publicly fetchable without login from a stable URL that sends CORS headers (archive.org `/download/` does: `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, checked 2026-11-24).
4. The review is re-run here, with a row per file set, before any UI is built.

Leads worth re-checking: (a) 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sa'id's Bukhari readings (#6), if published as a series under CC BY/BY-SA; (b) the original source of the widely re-uploaded Arabic 40 Nawawi recitation (#1–#3), whose reciter isn't identified, in case the rights holder publishes it under an open licence; (c) Wikimedia Commons, searched directly from an unrestricted network.
