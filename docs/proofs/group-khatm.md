# Proof log: Group khatm (NF-3.18)

- **Feature:** a `khatm_group` circle shares the Qur'an out in parts — 30 juz (default), 60 hizb or 114 surahs — with self-pick, round-robin or creator-assigns modes, a deadline, rounds, a stale-part reassign flow that asks the member first, and a calm completion card with a dua by reference.
- **Code:** `apps/app/src/lib/circles/khatm.ts`, `khatm-hooks.ts`, `apps/app/src/components/circles/khatm/*`, `apps/app/src/app/khatm-groups/*`; backend `apps/api/src/circles/group-khatm.*` (`docs/CIRCLES.md`).
- **Review:** AI proof-backed review (D11), 2026-11-23.
- **Scripture in the UI:** none typed. The completion card links to the bundled dua `quranic-taqabbal` (`/dua/detail/quranic-taqabbal`), whose Arabic, transliteration and translation come from the dataset (`packages/shared/src/content/duas.ts`, reference "Quran 2:127").

## 3. Intention note — gifting reward to the deceased (isal al-thawab)

| Claim                                                                           | Basis                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Copy                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Scholars differ on whether the reward of Qur'an recitation reaches the deceased | Well-known difference: the majority of Hanafis and Hanbalis (and later Shafi'is and Malikis) hold that it reaches when gifted; the classical position attributed to al-Shafi'i and Malik is that recitation's reward does not reach the deceased (dua and charity do, by consensus). Recorded in e.g. Ibn Qudamah, _al-Mughni_, Kitab al-Jana'iz (on gifting rewards to the dead), and al-Nawawi, _al-Adhkar_, bab ma yanfa' al-mayyit. | "Scholars differ on gifting the reward of recitation to the deceased; the app makes no claim either way." — neutral, no ruling asserted (plan NF-3.18 "Intention note"). |
